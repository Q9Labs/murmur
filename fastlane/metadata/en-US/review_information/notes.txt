Murmur is an accountless one-way live translator. No account, demo credentials, subscription, or 2FA are required.

Test path:
1. Open Murmur.
2. Choose English as source and Arabic as target.
3. Optionally switch between Phrase and Continuous mode.
4. Tap Listen and allow microphone access.
5. Speak a short phrase such as "Hello, how are you today?"
6. Confirm source captions and translated captions appear.
7. If speech output is configured, translated speech may play. If speech output is unavailable, captions continue and the app shows the degraded speech state.
8. In Continuous mode, background the app briefly during an active session, return to the app, and confirm the session is still active.
9. Tap Stop, then start again.
10. Tap Cancel during a session and confirm microphone capture/playback stop.
11. Use Report translation on a committed translated span.
12. Use Reset Murmur Identity and Delete Local Data from the app controls.

Murmur uses the microphone only during a user-started live translation session. The audio background mode is used only so an active live translation session can continue during app switches or lock-screen moments; Stop and Cancel end capture/playback. Murmur V1 has no account creation, login, profile, or cloud transcript history.
