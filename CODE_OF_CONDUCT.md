# Code of Conduct

Murmur contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's identity, contact details, credentials, or private communications.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms contributors or the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy, and take proportionate action.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
