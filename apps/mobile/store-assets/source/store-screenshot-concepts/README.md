# Store screenshot concepts

These are Imagegen direction studies, not upload-ready store files. They are 852×1846 previews. The generated phone panel must be replaced programmatically with an untouched native capture before export and validation.

All directions prohibit people, hands, silhouettes, lifestyle photography, flags, badges, ratings, watermarks, invented UI, and unsupported product claims.

- `concept-a-wave-editorial.png`: cream editorial frame, full Murmur color wave, headline “Read the talk live in your language.”
- `concept-b-dark-coral.png`: near-black campaign frame, coral field with thin teal and gold accents, headline “Translation, as it happens.”
- `concept-c-product-grid.png`: cream product grid, deep-plum field and four compact color bars, headline “Choose a direction. Tap Listen.”
