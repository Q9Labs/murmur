Follow spoken language as it happens.

Murmur turns speech into live translated captions, so you can keep up with a tour, conference talk, lecture, class, demo, or sermon in another language.

Choose a source language and target language, then tap Listen. Murmur shows translated captions as speech is recognized and can play translated speech when audio output is available.

Murmur supports English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, and Dutch — with right-to-left support for Arabic.

START FREE, KEEP YOUR TIME

Murmur creates a durable guest account with 10 translation minutes each month, so no sign-up is needed. Murmur Pro includes 3 hours each month, with monthly and annual plans. Non-expiring credit packs add time when you need it. You can link an email for recovery, restore Google Play purchases, and delete your account in the app.

Murmur does not save cloud transcript history by default. Microphone audio streams only during a live session, and you can stop or cancel at any time.

Choose Microphone for nearby speech or Device Audio on supported Android phones to translate eligible media playing in another app. Device Audio can show translated captions in a draggable bubble when you allow display over other apps. Some apps and protected media block playback capture.

Murmur needs an internet connection. Android requires audio-recording access for both capture modes and screen-sharing approval for each Device Audio session; Murmur does not record the microphone in Device Audio mode.

Murmur uses OpenAI Realtime for speech recognition, translation, and translated speech. AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not use Murmur as the only source for emergencies, medical, legal, financial, immigration, or other high-stakes decisions.
