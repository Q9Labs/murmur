Follow spoken language as it happens.

Murmur turns speech into live translated captions, so you can keep up with a tour, conference talk, lecture, class, demo, or sermon in another language.

Choose a source language and target language, then tap Listen. Use Phrase Mode for short, deliberate speech or Continuous Mode when someone keeps talking. Murmur shows translated captions as stable speech is recognized and can play translated speech when speech output is available.

Murmur supports English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, and Dutch — with right-to-left support for Arabic.

NO ACCOUNT OR SAVED TRANSCRIPT HISTORY

Murmur is accountless: there is no login, profile, or cloud transcript history. Microphone audio streams only during a live session. You can stop or cancel at any time, reset your anonymous Murmur identity, and delete local Murmur data from the app.

BUILT FOR READABLE LIVE TRANSLATION

• Live translated captions
• Phrase Mode for short speech
• Continuous Mode for tours, talks, lectures, and other ongoing speech
• Optional translated speech output
• Source and target language controls
• In-app reporting for translation issues

Murmur needs an internet connection and a microphone — that's it.

Murmur uses AI speech recognition, translation, and speech generation providers. AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not use Murmur as the only source for emergencies, medical, legal, financial, immigration, or other high-stakes decisions.
