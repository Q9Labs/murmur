Follow spoken language as it happens.

Murmur turns speech into live translated captions, so you can keep up with a tour, conference talk, lecture, class, demo, or sermon in another language.

Choose a source language and target language, then tap Listen. Murmur shows translated captions as speech is recognized and can play translated speech when audio output is available.

Murmur supports English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, and Dutch — with right-to-left support for Arabic.

NO ACCOUNT OR SAVED TRANSCRIPT HISTORY

Murmur has no accounts, login, profiles, or cloud transcript history. Microphone audio streams only during a live session. You can stop or cancel at any time, reset your anonymous Murmur identity, and delete local Murmur data from the app.

BUILT FOR READABLE LIVE TRANSLATION

• Live translated captions
• Live captions for short exchanges and ongoing speech
• Ordered translated captions as speech is recognized
• Optional translated speech output
• Source and target language controls
• In-app reporting for translation issues

Murmur needs an internet connection and a microphone — that's it.

Murmur uses OpenAI Realtime for speech recognition, translation, and translated speech. Translations can be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. Do not rely on Murmur for emergencies, medical, legal, financial, immigration, or other high-stakes decisions without a qualified human interpreter.
