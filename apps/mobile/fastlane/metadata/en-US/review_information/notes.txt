Murmur is a one-way live translator. It creates a durable guest account automatically, so no demo credentials or sign-in are required. A new guest install may include Free minutes depending on the current offer. If no time is available, tapping Listen opens the plans; buy Trip Pass with an Apple sandbox account, then follow the test path below. Optional Pro and Pro Max subscriptions and Trip Pass (60 minutes) or Event Pass (300 minutes) packs use Apple in-app purchase. Purchased pack minutes never expire.

Test path:
1. Open Murmur.
2. Choose English as source and Arabic as target.
3. Tap Listen and allow microphone access.
4. Speak a short sentence such as "Hello, how are you today?"
5. Confirm source captions and translated captions appear.
6. Confirm translated speech plays.
7. Background the app briefly during an active session, return to the app, and confirm the session is still active.
8. Tap Stop, then start again.
9. Tap Cancel during a session and confirm microphone capture/playback stop.
10. Use Report translation on a committed translated span.
11. Open Account & Billing. Add and verify an email address, then confirm the Pro, credit pack, and Restore Purchases controls become available.
12. Use an Apple sandbox purchase to test a product, then confirm the updated time appears after server verification.
13. Delete the Murmur account and confirm the app explains that account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Murmur uses the microphone only during a user-started live translation session. The audio background capability is used only so an active live translation session can continue during app switches or lock-screen moments; Stop and Cancel end capture/playback. Murmur does not retain microphone audio or cloud transcript history by default. Purchases are verified server-side through RevenueCat before Murmur grants translation time.
